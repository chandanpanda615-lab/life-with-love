getting here

There is no railway into Kandhamal. Most people fly into Bhubaneswar and drive — roughly 250 kilometres, most of a day, the last stretch climbing west into the Eastern Ghats. The nearest railhead is Berhampur, about 150 kilometres south, with onward options at Bhubaneswar, Sambalpur and Muniguda. However you come, the final approach is by road, from the south, past Baliguda. The drive is not incidental. It is where the plain gives way to forest, and it is the first thing the land tells you.

the village

Sarangada is a mixed village strung along about a kilometre of market road — Kondh, Brahmin, Kshatriya and Christian families sharing the same red soil. Odia in the market and the school, Kui in the older Kondh homes, Hindi among the young. Pincode 762106. 20.227°N, 84.124°E.

what to expect

Plain comforts, honestly. You sleep in the village, not beside it. The tea is too sweet. There is no wifi after seven, and nobody minds. Days begin before you are ready and end facing the trees.

who it's for

Anyone who wants a place as it is rather than as it is performed. If you came for a single culture staged for a camera, you will leave disappointed. If you came to be absorbed for seven days, this is that.

Why I am building this slowly

A village is not a product, and I would rather take two years than get this wrong.

Seven days. One village. Not open yet.

This is a pilot in design. The route, the host model, and what the village is willing to share are still being settled — in the village, before they are settled on a website. Nothing here is bookable, and no date is being promised.
