# Maati Katha — Truth Audit

*Created: 5 May 2026. Use this before publishing, pitching, or expanding the website.*

This file separates what is already verified from what is only an idea, a draft, or a sensitive claim. The rule is simple: public copy can only use `TRUE` claims directly. `LIKELY` and `NOT YET` claims can shape research, but should not be sold as facts. `DO NOT SAY` claims should stay off the website unless they are rewritten with context and consent.

## Status key

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `TRUE` | Personally verified, sourced, or grounded in settled project material. Safe to use with care. |
| `LIKELY` | Plausible and useful, but needs a named source, fieldwork note, map, interview, or document. |
| `NOT YET` | A good project idea or itinerary draft, but not operationally proven. Do not promise it. |
| `DO NOT SAY` | False, too broad, ethically risky, or likely to misrepresent Sarangada or another community. |

## Fieldwork Checklist Before Public Launch

- Confirm the host household and who is responsible for each guest-facing task.
- Ask who in the village is comfortable being photographed, named, interviewed, or left out.
- Map the main spine, pond, public institutions, field paths, and western route with GPS.
- Identify what guests must never photograph: private homes, rituals, children, sacred spaces, conflict-sensitive sites.
- Confirm the turmeric walk with exact village names, distance, season, host, and payment.
- Document transport from Baliguda/Berhampur, medical access, phone coverage, and emergency plan.
- Decide where guests sleep, bathe, eat, charge devices, and store valuables.
- Run one private pilot with two trusted guests before accepting strangers.
